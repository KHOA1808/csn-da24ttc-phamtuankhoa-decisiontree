# Tài liệu tham khảo

Danh sách tài liệu được trích dẫn trong báo cáo (định dạng IEEE, xếp theo thứ tự ABC).

1. S. Aeberhard and M. Forina, "Wine," *UCI Machine Learning Repository*, 1991. https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/109/wine
2. L. Breiman, "Random forests," *Machine Learning*, vol. 45, no. 1, pp. 5–32, 2001.
3. L. Breiman, J. H. Friedman, R. A. Olshen, and C. J. Stone, *Classification and Regression Trees*. Belmont, CA, USA: Wadsworth, 1984.
4. T. Chen and C. Guestrin, "XGBoost: A scalable tree boosting system," in *Proc. 22nd ACM SIGKDD*, 2016, pp. 785–794.
5. T. Fawcett, "An introduction to ROC analysis," *Pattern Recognition Letters*, vol. 27, no. 8, pp. 861–874, 2006.
6. R. A. Fisher, "The use of multiple measurements in taxonomic problems," *Annals of Eugenics*, vol. 7, no. 2, pp. 179–188, 1936.
7. J. H. Friedman, "Greedy function approximation: A gradient boosting machine," *The Annals of Statistics*, vol. 29, no. 5, pp. 1189–1232, 2001.
8. J. Han, M. Kamber, and J. Pei, *Data Mining: Concepts and Techniques*, 3rd ed. Morgan Kaufmann, 2011.
9. T. Hastie, R. Tibshirani, and J. Friedman, *The Elements of Statistical Learning*, 2nd ed. Springer, 2009.
10. G. V. Kass, "An exploratory technique for investigating large quantities of categorical data," *Applied Statistics*, vol. 29, no. 2, pp. 119–127, 1980.
11. R. Kohavi, "A study of cross-validation and bootstrap for accuracy estimation and model selection," in *Proc. IJCAI*, 1995, pp. 1137–1145.
12. T. M. Mitchell, *Machine Learning*. McGraw-Hill, 1997.
13. F. Pedregosa et al., "Scikit-learn: Machine learning in Python," *JMLR*, vol. 12, pp. 2825–2830, 2011.
14. J. R. Quinlan, "Induction of decision trees," *Machine Learning*, vol. 1, no. 1, pp. 81–106, 1986.
15. J. R. Quinlan, *C4.5: Programs for Machine Learning*. Morgan Kaufmann, 1993.
16. scikit-learn developers, "Decision Trees," *scikit-learn User Guide*. https://scikit-learn.org/stable/modules/tree.html
17. C. E. Shannon, "A mathematical theory of communication," *The Bell System Technical Journal*, vol. 27, no. 3, pp. 379–423, 1948.
18. Vũ Hữu Tiệp, *Machine Learning cơ bản*, 2018. https://machinelearningcoban.com/ebook/
19. W. H. Wolberg, O. L. Mangasarian, N. Street, and W. N. Street, "Breast Cancer Wisconsin (Diagnostic)," *UCI Machine Learning Repository*, 1995. https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/17/breast+cancer+wisconsin+diagnostic
